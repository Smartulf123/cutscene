# Caption font licences

The caption fonts in `../files/` (and inlined in `../fonts.css`) are the Latin subsets published
by Fontsource (https://fontsource.org, https://github.com/fontsource/font-files) from Google
Fonts. Each family's licence, with its copyright line, is kept here as Fontsource ships it:

- `OFL-<family>.txt`: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (22 families: Anton, Audiowide, Baloo 2, Bangers,
  Bodoni Moda, Caveat, Chakra Petch, Cinzel, Cormorant Garamond, Creepster, Fredoka, Inter,
  Monoton, Orbitron, Press Start 2P, Rajdhani, Saira Stencil One, Shippori Mincho, Sora, Space
  Grotesk, Teko, VT323).
- `LICENSE-<family>.txt`: Apache License 2.0 (Permanent Marker, Special Elite).

The fonts are unchanged apart from being the Latin subset. Under the OFL they may be used,
embedded in videos and redistributed, but not sold on their own.
